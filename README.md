# Welcome to Sunset Royale

## Beach Access

The beach is a 5 minute walk.  When leaving the community, cross the street and take a left. About 50 feet up you will come to a path on your right. Take that. The path will branch left and right - take the left branch. This will drop you in a cul de sac which in turn leads to a beach access boardwalk.

## Community pool, gym and tennis/pickleball

Make a left on NC12 going north. The Sanderling swim and racquet club is about .5 miles north.  Just tell them your are staying at 115 Royal Tern.

## TV

The upstairs TV needs three remotes to operate!  The Samsung controller turns the TV on - select HDM1 as input. Use the Yamaha controller to control the soundbar - make sure the input is on TV.  The Roku controller controls the streaming device.  To access basic cable, choose the Spectrum app via the Roku.


## Kayak

There is a dolly in the back room that can be used to get the kayak to the sound. Please use that as dragging it causes damage. Also, only use the kayak in the sound as it is not designed to be used in the ocean.

## Cornhole

Please bring the boards in when you are finished - they donn't do well in the rain.
